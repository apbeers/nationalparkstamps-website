Accessibility

National Park Stamps is designed to be usable by as many people as possible. We aim to make the app easy to understand, easy to navigate, and comfortable to use for people with varied abilities.

Accessibility Features

The app supports key Apple accessibility features on iOS, including:

VoiceOver

Screen reader support for users who navigate the app using spoken feedback.

Voice Control

Hands-free navigation and app control using voice commands.

Larger Text

Text can be displayed at larger sizes for easier reading.

Dark Interface

Dark mode support for comfortable low-light viewing.

Reduced Motion

Motion-sensitive animations and transitions are minimized where possible.

High Contrast

Text and controls are designed to remain legible and distinguishable.

Accessible Controls

Buttons and interactive elements are designed to be clear and easy to target.

Dynamic Type Support

The app is designed to work with system text sizing preferences.

Our Commitment

Inclusive design matters. We believe accessibility is a core part of a quality app experience and we continue to improve the app based on usability and feedback.

Accessibility and App Behavior

National Park Stamps uses the device's built-in accessibility features and standard iOS interfaces. The app does not require any special hardware beyond standard iPhone or iPad accessibility settings and controls.

We work to ensure that visual information is not communicated by color alone, that content remains readable, and that essential actions can be used without requiring fine motor precision.

Feedback and Improvements

We welcome feedback about accessibility. If you encounter a barrier, usability issue, or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at apbeers@icloud.com.
