Accessibility

National Park Stamps is committed to accessibility for all users. Our app is designed with inclusive features to ensure that everyone can enjoy exploring and documenting national park visits, regardless of their abilities.

Supported Apple Accessibility Features

The National Park Stamps app supports all of the following Apple accessibility features on iOS:

VoiceOver

Screen reader for visually impaired users to navigate and interact with the app.

Voice Control

Control the app hands-free using spoken commands and voice navigation.

Larger Text

Adjust text size throughout the app for easier reading and reduced eye strain.

Dark Interface

Reduce screen brightness and blue light for comfortable viewing in low-light environments.

Differentiate Without Color Alone

Visual elements are distinguishable without relying solely on color differences.

Sufficient Contrast

Text and interactive elements meet accessibility standards for visual contrast.

Reduced Motion

Minimize animations and transitions for users sensitive to motion.

Captions

Support for closed captions and visual indicators for audio content.

Audio Descriptions

Detailed audio descriptions of important visual elements and interactions.

Our Commitment

Inclusive Design. We believe everyone should have equal access to the National Park Stamps experience. Our app is built with accessibility in mind from the ground up.

Feedback and Improvements

We are continually working to improve the accessibility of our app. If you encounter any accessibility issues or have suggestions for improvements, we encourage you to reach out through the App Store. Your feedback helps us create a better experience for all users.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about accessibility features or need assistance using National Park Stamps, please contact us through the App Store.
