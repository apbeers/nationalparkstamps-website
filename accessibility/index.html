Accessibility

Every journey deserves a way in.

We aim to make National Park Memories & Tracker easy to understand, navigate, and enjoy for people with varied abilities.

Accessibility commitment

Accessibility is a core part of a quality app experience. We work to make essential actions clear, information readable, and navigation comfortable across the iPhone accessibility tools people already use.

Designed to work with iPhone

  • VoiceOver and Voice Control: support for spoken feedback and hands-free navigation.
  • Dynamic Type: text can adapt to larger system text sizes.
  • Dark appearance: a comfortable option for lower-light viewing.
  • Reduced motion: motion-sensitive transitions can be minimized where possible.
  • Clear controls: buttons and actions are designed to be distinguishable and easy to target.

Our approach

We work to avoid relying on color alone, preserve readable contrast, and make important tasks usable without requiring fine motor precision. As the app evolves, we will continue to improve based on feedback.

If something makes the app harder to use, we want to know. Your feedback directly helps improve the experience.
