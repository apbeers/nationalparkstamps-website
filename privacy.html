Privacy Policy

National Park Stamps is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and use in our iOS application.

Data Collection

National Park Stamps does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. We do not track your activity, gather analytics, or store any personal information on our servers.

Photo Metadata

The National Park Stamps application scans photo metadata on your device to extract and identify national park information. This processing occurs entirely on your device and no data is transmitted to our servers or any third parties. The scanned data remains under your control and is not shared, stored remotely, or used for any purpose other than the app's functionality.

Third-Party Services

National Park Stamps does not use any third-party libraries, services, or analytics tools. We do not share your information with any external parties.

Your Privacy

Your data is yours. All processing happens on your device. Nothing is transmitted from your device to our servers or any other service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and we encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through the App Store.
