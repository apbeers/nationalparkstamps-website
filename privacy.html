Privacy Policy

National Park Stamps respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information and what data is and is not collected.

Information We Do Not Collect

National Park Stamps does not collect, store, or transmit personal data from the app. We do not sell user data, build advertising profiles, or use third-party analytics to track your usage.

We do not store your name, email address, account information, location history, or browsing activity in connection with the app. We do not use external analytics services or advertising SDKs.

Photo Metadata Processing

The app may read image metadata stored on your device to identify national park information associated with a photo. This processing occurs entirely on your device. We do not upload the image, the metadata, or any other private content to our servers.

The app may use information already stored on the device to help interpret photo metadata and match it to park locations or related data. That processing is local to the device and remains under your control.

Permissions

The app may request access to your photo library so it can analyze photos already stored on your device. This access is used only for the intended app feature and is not used to collect or share your personal information.

Third-Party Services

National Park Stamps does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or background data collection services. No user data is shared with external parties for marketing or tracking purposes.

Your Privacy

Your data stays on your device. Photos, metadata, and app processing remain local to the device unless you explicitly choose to share content outside the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the app after a change is posted indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at apbeers@icloud.com.
