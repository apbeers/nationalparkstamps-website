Common Questions

How does the app work?

National Park Stamps helps identify park information from photo metadata already stored on your device. All relevant processing happens locally on the device.

Is my data safe?

Yes. The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal data for analytics or marketing purposes. Please review the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Does the app support accessibility features?

Yes. The app is designed to work with major iOS accessibility features such as VoiceOver, Voice Control, larger text, dark mode, and reduced motion support.

Why does the app ask for Photos access?

Photos access allows the app to read metadata from images already stored on your device so it can identify park-related information relevant to those photos.

How do I report a bug or accessibility issue?

Please email us at apbeers@icloud.com with a description of the problem and, if possible, the iOS version and device model.
