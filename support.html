Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use National Park Stamps?

National Park Stamps analyzes photos from your device to extract metadata and identify national park information. Open the app, select photos from your library, and the app will process them on your device to identify parks and provide information.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All processing happens entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, photos, or location information. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

What accessibility features are supported?

National Park Stamps supports VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, Dark Interface, Reduced Motion, Captions, and many other Apple accessibility features. Visit our Accessibility page for complete information.

Why did the app ask for photo library access?

The app needs access to your photo library to read metadata from your photos. This permission is required to identify national park information from your images. No data is collected or shared.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! All processing happens on your device, so you can use National Park Stamps without an internet connection.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We'd love to hear your feedback! Email us at apbeers@icloud.com with details about bugs or feature suggestions.

What should I do if I encounter accessibility issues?

If you experience any accessibility problems, please contact us at apbeers@icloud.com. We're committed to making our app accessible to everyone and appreciate your feedback.
