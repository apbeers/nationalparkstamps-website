About National Park Stamps

National Park Stamps is a simple, privacy-conscious app for exploring and identifying national park information from your photos. It helps you connect memories with places and provides an easy way to learn more about the parks you visit.

Key Features

📸 Photo-based identification

Review photos stored on your device and identify park-related information from built-in metadata.

🏞️ Park discovery

Explore park details associated with your images and learn more about the places you visit.

🔒 Privacy first

Processing is performed locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal user data.

♿ Accessibility focused

The app is designed to work with iOS accessibility features for a more inclusive experience.

🌐 Offline-friendly

Core app functionality works without an internet connection.

📱 iOS-friendly design

Optimized for a clean, intuitive mobile experience with system-native styling.

Why People Use It

How It Works

The app reads photo metadata already stored on the device and uses that information to help identify park-related context. This process is local and remains under the user's control.

  • Open the app and select your photos.
  • Allow the app to read the photo metadata already available on the device.
  • Review the associated park information for each selected image.

Accessibility

National Park Stamps was created with accessibility in mind. The app supports standard iOS accessibility features including VoiceOver, larger text, dark mode, and reduced motion, helping make the experience more usable for a wide range of people.

For more detailed accessibility information, please visit the Accessibility page.
