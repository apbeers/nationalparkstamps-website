About National Park Stamps

National Park Stamps is an innovative iOS app designed to help you identify national parks from photos and preserve memories of your outdoor adventures. Whether you're a seasoned park explorer or discovering these natural wonders for the first time, our app makes it easy to document and organize your park visits.

Key Features

📸 Photo Analysis

Automatically analyze photos to extract metadata and identify national park information with intelligent processing.

🏞️ Park Identification

Discover which national parks are featured in your photos through advanced metadata recognition.

🔒 Privacy First

All processing happens on your device. We never collect, store, or transmit your personal data or photos.

♿ Fully Accessible

Complete support for VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, and all major Apple accessibility features.

🌐 Offline Capable

Use the app anywhere, anytime. No internet connection required for core functionality.

📱 Beautiful Design

Intuitive interface optimized for iOS with support for Light and Dark modes.

How It Works

National Park Stamps makes it simple to document your park visits:

  • Open the App: Launch National Park Stamps from your home screen
  • Select Photos: Choose photos from your camera roll that you'd like to analyze
  • Get Insights: The app identifies national park information embedded in photo metadata
  • Preserve Memories: Organize and track your national park adventures

For Nature Enthusiasts

Perfect for park lovers and outdoor adventurers. Whether you're collecting stamps from every park, planning your next trip, or simply want to remember your favorite destinations, National Park Stamps is your companion for every adventure.

Permissions

National Park Stamps requests access to your photo library to analyze photos on your device. This permission is required for the app to function and extract national park metadata from your images. No data is collected or shared with external services.

Accessibility at the Core

We believe that everyone should be able to enjoy National Park Stamps. The app is built with accessibility in mind from the ground up, supporting:

  • VoiceOver screen reader for visually impaired users
  • Voice Control for hands-free navigation
  • Support for Larger Text and Dynamic Type
  • Dark Interface option for comfortable viewing
  • Reduced Motion support
  • High contrast text and visual elements
  • Captions and audio descriptions

For complete accessibility details, visit our Accessibility page.
