Privacy promise

Your photos stay yours.

National Park Memories & Tracker was made to help you revisit your adventures—not to collect your data.

Privacy Policy

National Park Memories & Tracker respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information and what data is—and is not—collected.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data from the app. We do not sell data, build advertising profiles, or use third-party analytics to track how you use the app.

We do not store your name, email address, account information, location history, or browsing activity in connection with the app.

Photo and location processing

The app may read photo metadata stored on your device to identify national parks connected to a photo. This processing happens entirely on your device. We do not upload your images, metadata, or other private content to our servers.

Photos, locations, and park data remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them outside the app.

Permissions

The app may request access to your photo library so it can analyze photos already stored on your device and organize park memories. That access is used only for the app’s intended features.

Third-party services

National Park Memories & Tracker does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or background data collection services. No user data is shared with external parties for marketing or tracking purposes.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here. Questions about privacy can be sent to apbeers@icloud.com.
